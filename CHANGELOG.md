> **Version 3.10 - Font flags, inform fixes, and polish**
>
> Version 3.10 adds configurable **Font Flags** in **Appearance**, including Slug presets applied across the main display, previews, **Mob Percentages** and **Progress Bar** callouts. This update also fixes **Inform Group** chat messages showing an extra percent sign, keeps the **Progress Bar** hidden after leaving a dungeon, corrects **Algeth'ar Academy** route data, and refreshes the Russian translation.

- 🆕 _**NEW** -_ Added configurable **Font Flags** in **Appearance**, with presets such as Outline, Slug, and Outline + Slug applied across the main display, previews, **Mob Percentages**, **Progress Bar** callouts, and import/export dialogs. Outline + Slug is the default when unset.
- 🛠️ _**IMPROVEMENT** -_ Russian translation updated, thank you **Hollicsh**.
- 🐞 _**BUGFIX** -_ Fixed **Inform Group** chat messages showing a trailing extra percent sign (e.g. "34.15% %") when using /say, /party, or /yell.
- 🐞 _**BUGFIX** -_ Fixed the **Progress Bar** staying visible after leaving a Mythic+ dungeon instead of hiding with the main display.
- 🐞 _**BUGFIX** -_ Fixed **Algeth'ar Academy** route ranks and trash percentages by correcting the Crawth and Overgrown Ancient boss order in dungeon data.
